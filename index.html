FAQ

Frequently asked

Which territories do you cover?

All of Canada and the United States — local, interprovincial and cross-border. Whether it's a single lane or a complex route, we identify the best solution to deliver your goods quickly and safely.

What transport solutions do you offer?

Road, sea and air transport, plus turnkey 4PL logistics. Equipment options include Drybox, Flat-Bed and Specialized services.

Do you provide real-time tracking?

Yes. We provide real-time shipment tracking so you always know where your goods are, and our team stays available at every step.

What types of clients do you work with?

SMEs, independent professionals and large corporations — every client receives the same high level of service, whatever the size of the business.

How quickly do you respond?

We generally reply within an hour during business hours, and as quickly as possible outside of them.
