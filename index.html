Equipment

Flatbed & Drybox, in detail

Two complementary options — we select the right one for your cargo.

Flat-Bed

An open deck for freight that doesn't fit in a box — loaded from the sides or above, and secured to the frame.

  • Construction materials — lumber, steel, drywall
  • Machinery and heavy equipment
  • Oversized freight handling
  • Professional securement and tarps

Drybox

A sealed, enclosed trailer for freight that needs to arrive exactly as it left — protected from the road and the weather.

  • Palletized and boxed goods
  • Full weather protection
  • Sealed-trailer security
  • Local and cross-border delivery
