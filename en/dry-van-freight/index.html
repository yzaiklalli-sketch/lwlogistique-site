01 / THE CARGO

What goes
in a dry van.

A dry van is an enclosed, non-refrigerated trailer loaded from the rear at a dock. Your freight travels protected from rain, snow and dust.

How many pallets fit in a 53' dry van?+

Usually up to 26 standard pallets on the floor, depending on size and total weight.

Do you handle partial loads?+

We specialize in full truckloads. Call us for special cases.

Do you move refrigerated freight?+

No, dry vans aren't refrigerated. They suit freight that doesn't need temperature control.

What do you need for a quote?+

Origin, destination, pallet count and size, total weight, type of goods and target date.
