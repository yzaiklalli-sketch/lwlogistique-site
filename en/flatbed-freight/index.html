01 / THE CARGO

What goes
on a flatbed.

A flatbed is open: it loads from the side, from above or by forklift. Freight is secured with straps or chains, and tarped when needed.

What's the difference between a flatbed and a step deck?+

A step deck has a lower rear deck, so taller freight can move without exceeding legal height.

Does my freight need to be tarped?+

It depends on the freight. Tell us in your request and we'll book a carrier with the right tarps.

Do you handle oversize loads?+

Call us at 450-436-0852 with dimensions and weight: we'll assess the equipment and permits required.

What do you need for a quote?+

Origin, destination, dimensions, weight, number of pieces, loading method and target date.
