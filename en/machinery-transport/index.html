What do you need to quote a machine?+

Dimensions (length, width, height), weight, loading method (forklift, crane, ramp), origin, destination and date. Photos help a lot.

My machine is over legal dimensions: what now?+

Call us at 450-436-0852. We'll assess the equipment (step deck or lowboy) and the permits needed for each province or state crossed.

Who loads and unloads the machine?+

Loading and unloading are done on site by the shipper and receiver (forklift, crane or ramp). Tell us what's available and we'll factor it into the equipment choice.

Do you move machinery to the US?+

Yes. For imports and exports, we coordinate with your customs broker.
